# CEO Brief — INTENT as life's co-pilot

## Vision

Most people cannot synthesise the knowledge that would change their lives
— it's scattered across podcasts, books, and eight siloed apps that each
own one organ. INTENT's job is to be the **co-pilot through life**: one
place that holds the evidence base, asks the minimum, builds the plan,
schedules the actions into a real week, and then learns from what
actually happens — optimising toward the user's own stated goals. Not
time blocking: *synthesis into action*. A better week, compounding into
a better life, for people who could never have assembled it themselves.

## What the silos teach us (research, Aug 2026)

| Competitor | What they've proven | What we adopt |
|---|---|---|
| **Noom** | A long personalised assessment *before* the paywall converts >10% of quiz completers vs 2.7% median — the "watch your program build" moment is the conversion event | Interview v4 is our assessment; the plan-review screen ("Paths starting today") is our program-build moment; the paywall belongs immediately after it |
| **Paywall research** | Value-first onboarding (3–5 screens of demonstrated value) converts 40–60% better than paywall-at-launch; 17–32-day trials convert ~46%, nearly double 3–7-day trials | Free tier must include a *complete* first week — the aha is a lived week, not a screenshot; long trial, not a hard gate |
| **Whoop / Rise** | Daily engagement comes from one morning readout the user *checks* — strain/recovery, sleep debt — a number that's theirs | Our morning readout is the momentum line + coach note on Today: what this week has done, what today serves, why |
| **Headspace / Calm** | Daily content loops retain; sessions are the habit | Our sessions (breathe, journal, meals, workout, review) + a rotating daily coach insight from the knowledge base |
| **Fabulous / Duolingo** | Ritual stacking and streaks drive habit — but streaks punish real life | We adopt the *ritual stack* (the Sunday reset: meal sketch + money check-in + week preview) and **reject streaks** for "don't miss twice" and weeks-won momentum — recovery-first is our moat, not our weakness |
| **YNAB** | A method, not a tracker, is what people pay for ("give every dollar a job") | Every path states its method in one line (automate-first; protein-first; shrink-don't-cancel) |
| **Strong / Fitbod** | Pre-decided workouts remove the decision at the gym door | Already ours (buildWorkout); extend the same "decided before you arrive" doctrine to every modality |

**The structural gap none of them fill:** each silo knows one domain and
nothing about the user's actual calendar, family, capacity, or other
goals. INTENT holds the seam: one evidence base + one calendar + one
learning loop across all domains. That is the defensible position.

## Monetization (design now, wire later — docs/MONETIZATION.md)

- **Free**: full interview, one active path, Today/Plan/adaptation, all
  sessions, 7-day-week experience complete. The free tier must be good
  enough to prove the product in one lived week.
- **INTENT Plus**: all five paths concurrently, AI narrative coach,
  household (partner sync, date-night coordination, babysitter pings),
  integrations (calendar, HealthKit), full history & weekly reports.
- **Placement**: one moment only — right after "Paths starting today"
  renders (our Noom moment), with a long trial. Never interrupt a
  session, never gate recovery features (urge help stays free, always —
  we don't monetise someone's worst moment).

## Action plan (this round)

1. **Protocol expansion** (+5, evidence-calibrated): mobility/stability,
   cold-shower finish, caffeine cutoff, protein-first breakfast, Sunday
   week preview (the ritual-stack anchor).
2. **Daily utility readout on Today**: momentum line (things done this
   week, goals moved) + one rotating "coach note" — the why behind
   something on today's plan, from the knowledge base. Whoop's morning
   check, without a wearable.
3. **Upgrade preview screen** (honest SCAFFOLDED — no payments): states
   the free/Plus split and the promise, linked from Settings, so the
   paywall arc is designed into the product before billing exists.
4. Docs: this brief + MONETIZATION.md.

## Later rounds (ordered)

Weekly report artifact (shareable wins recap) · path check-in prompts
that write back to milestones · household/partner layer · calendar +
HealthKit (the "7 real days" native milestone) · AI coach on the paid
tier · referral loop (family-first growth, per the founding intent).
