# Nutrition — week of Monday 9 November 2026

For Isaac. 90 kg, fat loss while keeping muscle, training four times
plus Monday futsal. **Protein 165–180 g a day, fibre 30 g.** No
logging, no calorie counting — the only number this plan keeps is
protein, because it is the one that protects muscle in a deficit.

The dinners and the shopping list below are generated from the repo's
own `DISHES` library and `buildShoppingList`
(`npm run isaac:meals`), not invented.

**Protein figures are my estimates from ordinary portion sizes.** They
are good to about ±5 g. Nothing here needs weighing.

**Running total: ~173 g.** Lands it.

**Why mackerel tonight and not something better.** You get home at
quarter past seven, hungry and tired, which is the exact state in
which a 40-minute recipe becomes a takeaway. Tonight's dinner is
chosen to need **no cooking**. That is the whole reason it is on a
Monday.

**The 16:45 meal is the one that matters** and it is the one most
likely to get skipped. Futsal is repeated sprinting and runs on
glycogen. Eating less before it does not speed up fat loss; it just
makes you worse at futsal and hungrier at half nine — which, now the
boys are down at 20:00–20:30 rather than 19:00, is exactly the ninety
minutes this plan exists to protect.

**Avoid between 16:45 and kick-off:** chilli, fish sauce, a big raw
salad, anything fatty. All fine at lunch. Not 90 minutes before you
sprint.

Three meals and one snack. Same skeleton all week, so there is nothing
to decide on a Tuesday.

| | time | roughly | protein |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Breakfast** | 06:45 | 250 g Greek yoghurt + berries + 30 g seeds or nuts, **and** either 2 eggs or a scoop of whey | **~48 g** |
| **Lunch** | 12:30 | last night's dinner, or 2 tins tuna with beans, rocket, lemon, olive oil | **~55 g** |
| **Snack** | 15:00 | 2 Mayver's bars, or yoghurt, or an apple with nuts | **~21 g** |
| **Dinner** | 18:00 | the dish for that night, below | **~50 g** |

**= about 174 g.** Breakfast is the meal people under-build; at 48 g
it is doing a third of the day's work and takes five minutes.

**Fibre to 30 g** comes out of this without trying: seeds at
breakfast, beans or a tin at lunch, two veg at dinner, an apple. Four
sources, no counting.

## Saturday — the wedding

It is one of your two named events for November. Three things, and
none of them is about willpower:

1. **Eat properly before you go.** The tuna-and-bean salad at lunch,
   protein-forward, plus something at about 4pm. Saving calories for
   the evening is the single most reliable way to get drunk fast and
   eat badly at eleven.
2. **The vape stays at home.** Decided now, not in a function room.
   This is the hole in the event allowance — a drinking night is a
   vaping night unless the vape is physically elsewhere.
3. **Sunday is a normal day**, not a recovery day. The roast is in the
   plan for Sunday evening precisely so that Sunday does not become a
   write-off.

## Sunday — the thirty minutes that make the week work

Do these in this order. The roast is in the oven for most of it.

1. Put the chicken in. It needs no attention for an hour.
2. **Cook the week's protein while it roasts** — a tray of chicken
   thighs or a batch of the turkey bolognese. This is the single
   highest-value half hour in the week.
3. Portion five lunches: leftover roast, or tuna, beans and greens.
4. Big tub of Greek yoghurt into the fridge. That is breakfast solved
   five times.
5. **Set next Monday's slow-cooker beef stew out on the bench** so
   Monday morning is one action. Next Monday you walk in from futsal
   to a finished dinner instead of a 10-minute one.

## What this does not know

- **Protein numbers are estimates** from normal portions, ±5 g. No
  weighing required and none intended.
- **No calorie target anywhere.** For fat loss at 90 kg with this much
  training you are likely somewhere near 2,000–2,200, but the plan
  deliberately does not count — the protein floor and the 19:30
  kitchen close do the work, and a tally is a thing to fail.
- **It will not appear in the app.** These are files in the repo.
  Nothing here writes to the store (`intent-os-store`), and nothing in
  this container can reach the phone. Getting a plan in there needs an
  import path that does not exist.
- **There is no pre-training protocol in the library.** All 323
  searched. The Monday fuelling above is standard sports nutrition and
  carries no grade from this repo.
- **The dish amounts are for two.** The library says so itself.
- **The fibre figure is not tracked**, just sourced. If you want it
  measured, that is a different kind of plan and probably a worse one.
