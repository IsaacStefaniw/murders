## 3. Do free tool/calculator pages rank and earn links in health/productivity?

Case studies with numbers:
- Examine.com (Ahrefs case study, 28 Apr 2023): >1M monthly Google visits with no outreach; single L-theanine page >1,000 backlinks; dozens of links from The Guardian; 2018 core update cut ~90% of traffic, with ~90% of what remained branded — https://ahrefs.com/blog/examine-seo-case-study/
- Ahrefs "stats page" link-building case study: 515 outreach emails → 36 backlinks from 32 sites, ranked #1 for "seo statistics" — https://ahrefs.com/blog/link-building-case-study/
- HubSpot Website Grader "earned thousands of backlinks"; CoSchedule Headline Analyzer 1,200 referring domains — https://seoplaybook.ai/blog/free-tools-and-calculators-for-backlinks (secondary source; original HubSpot/CoSchedule figures unverified)
- "102 calculator tools that have driven more than 4,000 backlinks" — vendor claim on a Gumroad product page, https://oliverseo.gumroad.com/l/calculator-tools (marketing copy, low reliability)
- tdeecalculator.net: #1 in US for "tdee calculator", 214.1K monthly visits from that keyword alone; ~1.6M–1.81M visits/month (Aug 2026 / Dec); ~2,000 backlinks since 2015; traffic 48.9% direct, 41.8% google — https://www.similarweb.com/website/tdeecalculator.net/ ; https://creativewidgets.io/blog/calculator-websites-seo ; https://www.semrush.com/website/tdeecalculator.net/overview/
- sleepyti.me: ~9,564 unique visitors/day, ~10,520 pageviews/day, US rank as high as 23,595 — https://www.statshow.com/www/sleepyti.me (third-party estimator)
- Sleep Foundation sleep calculator: exists, no-email, 8 citations — https://www.sleepfoundation.org/sleep-calculator ; ranking data unknown
- Strength Level standards: no SEO case study or traffic numbers found (unknown)
- Ahrefs, Backlinko, Moz, Siege Media: no calculator-specific numeric case study surfaced in searches beyond the items above (unknown)

Reading: single-purpose calculators on single-keyword domains (TDEE, sleepyti.me) have ranked #1 for years on tool queries; the pattern is "one tool = one head keyword", not "tool page ranks for many 'does X work' questions". Examine is the counter-example: a *reference database*, not a tool, earned links per intervention page.

Synthesis for IntentNorth: a "does cold plunge work" query is (a) a question-format informational health query — the exact class Pew found triggers a summary 60% of the time and BrightEdge finds at ~89–100% for treatment-style healthcare queries; (b) where an AIO shows, top-result CTR is down 34.5% (Apr 2025) -> 58% (Dec 2025) per Ahrefs, and users click ~half as often per Pew (8% vs 15%). Upside: being cited inside the AIO lifts organic CTR +35% (Seer). An Option A page therefore competes mainly for AIO citation, not blue-link clicks.

## One-paragraph summary

Option A has a partial incumbent: Examine.com (A–F grades, supplements/nutrition only, grades paywalled, ~0.5–1M visits/month, historically link-rich) plus Consensus (yes/possibly/mixed/no meter on academic papers, 20 free searches/month) and Cochrane (free, GRADE certainty in prose). No free site grades lifestyle *practices* (cold plunge, morning light, journaling, zone 2, sauna) with mechanism + safety. Option B has no incumbent for a planned-week audit; adjacent are Wheel-of-Life / life-audit scorecards (small sites, no email) and email-gated app funnels (Noom 113 screens, email one-third in). On discoverability: question-format health queries trigger AI Overviews ~58% (Ahrefs) to ~89% (BrightEdge healthcare) of the time, and where an AIO appears position-1 CTR is down 34.5% (Apr 2025) to 58% (Dec 2025) per Ahrefs, 8% vs 15% click rate per Pew; being cited inside the AIO recovers +35% organic CTR (Seer). Single-purpose calculators still rank #1 for years (tdeecalculator.net: 214K visits/month from one keyword, ~2,000 backlinks). AU launched AIO 28–29 Oct 2024; no sourced AU prevalence figure exists.
