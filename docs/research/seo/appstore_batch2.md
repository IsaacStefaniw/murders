Collected 7 Sep 2026 from apps.apple.com/au via WebSearch + WebFetch. Nothing below is invented; fields that could not be read from the page are marked **unknown**.

## Important currency / reading notes

- On apps.apple.com/au the In-App Purchase list shows prices with a plain `$` symbol. On the AU storefront this is AUD (the page does not print "A$"). All IAP prices below are transcribed exactly as shown (`$`), and should be read as AUD.
- Several DESCRIPTION texts contain boilerplate prices in other currencies (Headspace quotes £, Calm/Balance/MacroFactor/Noom quote US$). These are noted separately and are NOT the AU IAP prices.
- The AU IAP lists often contain duplicate product names at different prices (regional/legacy SKUs). They are listed as-is.
- "Free trial" flags come from the description or IAP list only; mentions that appear only in user reviews are labelled as such.
- Character counts are for the exact Title / Subtitle strings, spaces included.
