# Training round — episodes and the credit ledger

## The corpus, and why every card here is uncredited

Gate 1 mined **nutrition and training together** from the shared index: 40 sources enumerated, 33 opened, 30 mined, 125 claims traced. Eleven came from the aggregator and nineteen from the shows' own sites, which is the ratio the access map predicted.

One stub recorded (a members-only preview) and **one source declined on conflict of interest**, an episode whose guest was the chief executive of a company selling the product under discussion.

## Two corrections to the corpus documents came out of using them

**A documented link was dead.** One of the topic-page slugs recorded in `../corpus/SOURCES.md` returns a 404. Corrected there.

**Clips inflate a pillar.** Of the 135 index rows routed to these two pillars, **91 are clips and shorts from four venues** rather than full conversations. Nutrition looks better covered than it is. Recorded in `../corpus/coverage.md`.

## Eleven of thirty mined sources sell what they describe

This is the field with the most of it, by some distance. Not disqualifying, per the roster, but the framing follows the product and the weightings are recorded in the ledger. Seven specific overstatements were logged, three of them from a single episode with a device affiliation.

## The finding that keeps repeating

The strongest evidence in this pillar — **219 randomised trials and 167,864 participants** on preventing falls — has **zero podcast coverage**. It is also the best card in the round.

Fifth pillar in a row. The pattern is recorded in `../corpus/PIPELINE.md`: the more rigorous the underlying science, the less likely a podcast covers it.

## One convergence, corrected

Discovery found six independent voices agreeing on the strength floor from what looked like four evidence bases. Verified separately, it is **two and a half**: prospective cohorts carry the finding, volume meta-regressions come second, the minimum-training review third and much smaller than its title, and **coaching practice carries none of the weight**.

The practical answer did not change. The confidence in it should.
