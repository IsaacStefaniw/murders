# Mind round — episodes and the credit ledger

Gate 1 mined **mind and skill together** from the shared corpus index: 48 episodes opened once and read for both pillars. The full index and all 126 traced claims are in `../gate1/mind-skill.md`; this file carries the mind slice and the credit verdicts.

## The best source was not on the aggregator

The single most valuable podcast source in this round is a full free transcript in the Tim Ferriss archive, with **Willoughby Britton** — the researcher the mind brief names as its top priority. Twenty-one thousand words, and the aggregator does not carry it.

That is the corpus map working: the aggregator covers the venues, the shows' own sites cover the shows, and for this pillar the shows' own sites won.

Her episode supplied three claims, all of which went to Gate 2. Two verified, one verified with a refinement, and **one did not exist**: there is no thirty-minute dose threshold anywhere in the literature. See `findings.md` section 2.

## Where the corpus is empty

Of the 434 index rows routed to mind or skill, **zero** cover behavioural activation, rumination, expressive writing, growth mindset, breathwork or nature exposure. Eleven of the briefs' named researchers return **zero episodes between them**, including Dimidjian, Pennebaker, Segal and Goldberg — several of whom wrote the papers this round's regrades rest on.

Third pillar in a row. The pattern is recorded in `../corpus/PIPELINE.md`.

**Consequence: six of seven cards ship with an empty attribution**, and one existing card's rationale changed without its grade changing, because the popular reasoning behind it turned out to be the unsupported one.
