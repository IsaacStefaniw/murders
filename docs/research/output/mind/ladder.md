# The Mind coach's ladder

A pillar is not a list of good practices, it is an order. This pillar has the strongest ordering claim in the library and it is currently implicit, which is a waste.

## The ordering claim, graded A

**Sleep first.**

When someone arrives with low mood, anxiety or rumination, the coach's opening move is to check whether sleep is the upstream problem. Not because sleep is more important in general, but because almost everything in this pillar works less well on top of a sleep debt, and because the sleep pillar has better evidence than most of what sits here.

This is not a card. It is the coach's first question, and it belongs in the knowledge base rather than on a schedule.

*Coach line:* "Before we add anything, how is the sleep? If that is the problem, we fix that first and half of this gets easier."

## Rung 1 — Do one small thing

`one-small-act` (existing, C, **proposed B**).

The most prominent card in the pillar, and after this round the evidence says so as well as the brief. Acting before you feel like acting is non-inferior to far more elaborate approaches, and it is the only thing here that reliably works when someone is too flat to do anything effortful.

Deliberately rung 1 and not meditation. Someone who cannot get out of the house is not going to be helped by being asked to sit with their attention.

*Coach line:* "Not because you feel like it. That is rather the point."

## Rung 2 — Give the difficult thing a container

`worry-window` (new, C), `write-it-three-times` (new, C), `evening-journal` (existing, B, **proposed C**).

Once someone is doing things again, the next problem is that the difficult material arrives at all hours. These cards do not remove it. They give it a time and a place, which is what stops it colonising the rest of the day.

Note that `write-it-three-times` is explicitly finite. It ends after three sessions and the card says so.

## Rung 3 — Change the tone

`compassion-break` (new, B), `talk-to-yourself-by-name` (new, C), `best-possible-self` (existing, C, **proposed B**), `gratitude-letter` (existing, C, **proposed B**).

Only worth reaching once rungs 1 and 2 are holding. All four are about how you address yourself, and all four are modest, cheap and repeatedly tested.

## Rung 4 — Sitting practice, with its safety card attached

`meditation-10` (existing, B, **proposed C**), `open-monitoring` (C), `loving-kindness` (C), and **`sitting-check` (new, C) is not optional here**.

Deliberately the last rung, which is close to the opposite of how this is usually sold. Meditation is the most demanded thing in the category and it is neither the best-evidenced nor the safest thing in this pillar. About one in ten people who meditate report an effect that got in the way of their life, and 60% of those reporting difficulties were teachers.

**`sitting-check` ships with any sitting practice, or the sitting practice does not ship.** That is a rule, not a preference.

*Coach line at the transition:* "Ten minutes, and one thing before we start: you can stop at any time, and telling me it is making things worse is a useful answer rather than a failure."

## Running alongside, not a rung

`two-hours-outside` (new, C) and `outdoor-reset` (existing, C) share a weekly total. `cyclic-sighing` (B) and `nsdr` (D) attach to whatever is already happening. `phone-parked` (D) belongs to attention rather than to mood, and the round corrected its rationale without changing its grade.

## What the coach says when someone is stuck

**Stuck at rung 1.** Make it smaller. The card's whole logic is that the action precedes the motivation, so the answer to "I could not face it" is a smaller thing, not a pep talk.

**Stuck and asking for meditation first.** Give it, and attach the safety card. Refusing what somebody asks for is how you lose them, and the ladder is a recommendation rather than a gate.

**Stuck because this is depression rather than a bad month.** Say so kindly and route. The best-evidenced thing in this entire round is a therapist-delivered relapse-prevention programme with a hazard ratio of 0.69, and the app must not deliver it. Naming the strongest option and saying it is not us is more trustworthy than quietly substituting a weaker one.

**In crisis.** Lifeline 13 11 14, 13YARN 13 92 76. Not a rung, not a card, and it should never be more than one tap away from anything in this pillar.
