# Connection & Family round — episodes and the credit ledger

62 URLs opened, **53 read from a real transcript, 50 distinct conversations**. Both inlets ran. 136 claim rows in Gate 1, all in our own words, no transcript text reproduced. Full index in `ledgers/gate1.md`.

## The index lied three times, and the corrections were substantive

This round is the reason `../corpus/coverage.md` now carries a corrections section.

**A name matched something else entirely.** The coverage table credited Sue Johnson with four aggregator episodes. All four are news items about people suing a pharmaceutical company with a similar name. She has **zero**. Her one real conversation is in the Tim Ferriss archive and turned out to be the most protocol-dense source in the round.

**Listed episodes served no transcript.** Esther Perel shows 20 and only **11** carry one — including the missing one that was the most worth having.

**Three episodes are misnamed by the index**, including the round's most important family-block source, attributed to the wrong Harvard psychologist. And one conversation is published twice under different titles, which reads as convergence and is not.

None of this changed the shape of the corpus. All of it changed how a search count should be treated: as a place to look.

## Concentration is the real limit here

| Measure | Value |

A pillar can look well-covered and be one venue's editorial voice repeated. That is worth knowing before treating 183 episodes as 183 independent sources.

## Where the corpus is empty

**Nine of the field's best-evidenced names return zero episodes**, against 183 routed on the topic: Holt-Lunstad, Gable, Reis, Fiese, Finkel, Deci, Ryan, Greene and one more.

Fourth pillar in a row. The literature inlet did not merely fill a gap this time, it produced the round's two headline findings — the loneliness split and the ceiling on self-directed relationship education. Neither is discussed by any communicator in the corpus.

## The rule paid for itself twice

Applying the author-list rule across the whole pillar, rather than only to the six flagged cards, **turned up five further failing credits** nobody had looked for. Two bibliography corrections were also needed, where a cited identifier resolved to a different first author than the credit assumed.

One live card is flagged separately: `teen-their-call` says "dozens of studies" and names none, and no supporting source could be located.
