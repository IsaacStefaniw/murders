# Nutrition round — episodes and the credit ledger

## The corpus

Gate 1 mined **nutrition and training together** from the shared index: 40 sources enumerated, 33 opened, 30 mined, 125 claims traced. Eleven came from the aggregator and nineteen from the shows' own sites.

One stub recorded, and **one source declined on conflict of interest**, an episode whose guest was the chief executive of a company selling the product under discussion.

**Clips inflate this pillar.** Of the 135 rows routed to these two pillars, 91 are clips and shorts from four venues rather than full conversations. Nutrition looks better covered than it is, and that is now recorded in `../corpus/coverage.md`.

## The names holding the credits, and the names holding the papers

**Seven of seven cards ship with an empty attribution.**

**Marie-Pierre St-Onge has zero credits in the library**, and several of this round's cards rest on her work. She satisfies both credit types at once on the fibre-and-sleep card: she is on the author list and she has taught it publicly. She should be added.

She is not alone at zero. So are van Loon, Nuckols, Kevin Hall, Lennon, Galpin, Lieberman, Israetel, Scheer, Garaulet and Jenkins.

Against that, three names hold **86 credits** between them.

That is the attribution problem in one line, and it is not that those three are wrongly credited. It is that the people who did the work are not credited at all.

## The claim that was withdrawn at Gate 2

Discovery reported that inconsistent time-restricted eating leaves people hungrier than not doing it at all, and it was relayed onward before verification.

It traces to a **conference-proceedings abstract authored by the company's own chief executive**, with no control group, self-reported data, and no peer-reviewed publication anywhere. Europe PMC was searched by title and by every author.

Not citeable, and no card rests on it. Recorded here because the failure was in the relaying rather than in the finding.

## The protein convergence, which was false

Four Tier 1 communicators state the same protein figure as settled. All four are quoting the same paper, accurately, from its abstract's closing sentence — a sentence the paper's own analysis does not support and whose discussion argues for considerably more.

Four apparently independent expert voices, one source, and the source is the one that erred first. Recorded in `sources.md` and `findings.md`, and stated fairly: this is enthusiasm rather than dishonesty, and it is precisely what a library that reads full texts is for.
