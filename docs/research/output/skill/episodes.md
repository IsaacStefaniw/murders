# Skill & Craft round — episodes and the credit ledger

Gate 1 mined **mind and skill together** from the shared corpus index: 48
episodes opened once and read for both pillars. The full episode index and
all 126 traced claims are in `../gate1/mind-skill.md`; this file carries
the skill slice and the credit verdicts.

## The corpus is empty exactly where this pillar is strongest

Of the 434 index rows routed to mind or skill, **zero** cover spaced
repetition, retrieval practice, deliberate practice, interleaving,
desirable difficulties, or learning styles. Eleven of the briefs' named
researchers return **zero episodes between them**, including Robert and
Elizabeth Bjork, Roediger, Karpicke and Rohrer — the people whose work the
pillar's two A grades rest on.

This is the third pillar in a row to find it. The pattern is now recorded
in `../corpus/PIPELINE.md`: the more rigorous the underlying science, the
less likely a podcast covers it.

**Consequence: five of six cards ship with an empty attribution.**
