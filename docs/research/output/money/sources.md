# Money round — source ledger

One row per candidate in `protocols.ts`, in file order, then the evidence behind the proposed regrades and copy fixes to the existing eleven money cards, then the items held or declined. Every peer-reviewed row was checked at record level on 8 September 2026: bibliographic details and retraction status against the Crossref registry record for the DOI, abstracts and figures against Europe PMC, PubMed Central, NBER, J-PAL, IDEAS/RePEc or the authors' pages, because publisher landing pages returned 403 to automated fetches on the day. Government pages were opened directly; rows that rest on an ato.gov.au page are marked **UNVERIFIED (403)** and their figures were corroborated only through search snippets of the named page. Those need a human click-through before any number ships. The three full working ledgers are in `ledgers/`.

Grade is the grade of the **practice** for an Australian app user, which can sit below the best study behind it.
