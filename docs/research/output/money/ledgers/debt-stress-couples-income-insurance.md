**What is settled (§1)**
- Interest arithmetic: highest-rate-first minimises total interest and time-to-zero for any fixed monthly payment. This is an identity, not a finding. The measured size of the penalty for ignoring it on real US portfolios is modest: +1.8–4.3% of interest paid (Hamilton 2023).
- Behavioural completion: three independent datasets (Gal & McShane 2012 field; Kettle et al. 2016 field + lab; Brown & Lahey 2015 lab) agree that concentrating extra payments on one balance and actually closing accounts keeps people going, and that the smallest balance gives the strongest progress signal. The only size available is institutional: ~14% higher elimination at 1 yr, ~43% by yr 4 for the "start small" profile (Kellogg Insight on Gal & McShane).
- Synthesis: (1) never disperse extra payments; (2) if smallest ≠ highest-rate, the interest cost of choosing smallest is small enough that completion plausibly dominates for people at risk of quitting, while avalanche dominates for people who will finish either way; (3) no trial has randomised people to snowball vs avalanche and followed them to zero. Say so.

**What survives (§2)**
- Debt is robustly associated with depression (OR≈2.8), any mental disorder (≈3.2) and suicide (≈5.8–7.9): A for association; causality runs both ways; never claim "paying off debt cures depression".
- Debt relative to assets prospectively predicts small physiological and psychological differences (+1 mmHg diastolic; +12–13% on stress/depression scales) in ~8,400 young adults: B.
- Financial strain tracks objectively worse sleep continuity in two PSG samples (n=75, n=368): C; a behavioural hook, not a headline number.
- "Poverty costs 13 IQ points" does not survive as stated. What survives: money worry can consume attention in anyone (Wicherts & Scholten's reading); the harvest-cycle within-person result is neither refuted nor independently replicated; the pooled poor-only differential is ≈0.09 SD with CI crossing zero. App wording: "money worry crowds out attention", never "13 IQ points".
