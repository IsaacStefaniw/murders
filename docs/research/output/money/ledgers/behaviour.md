# Behavioural mechanisms in personal saving — verified source ledger

Compiled 8 Sep 2026 for an evidence-graded practice library (Australian iPhone app). Sections 1–11 complete.

## How verification was done (read this before trusting any row)

Publisher landing pages (UChicago, Science, OUP, SAGE, INFORMS, Elsevier, Springer, Wiley, PNAS) returned HTTP 403 to automated fetches on the day. For every paper, bibliographic facts — title, authors, journal, volume, issue, pages, year — and the presence/absence of a Crossref "update-to"/retraction relation were therefore verified against the Crossref registry record for the DOI (api.crossref.org/works/<doi>), which is the DOI system of record. Abstracts and numbers were then verified on open mirrors: Europe PMC REST (PMID/PMCID), PubMed Central full text, NBER, IPA / J-PAL, IDEAS/RePEc, IZA, Rotman, Semantic Scholar, or the authors' institutional pages. Where a specific number could only be found in a secondary summary, the row says so and the number should be treated as **secondary**. Rows or figures marked **UNVERIFIED** were not confirmed on any landing page and must not be quoted in the app.

"Retraction status: none" = no Crossref update-to/retraction relation as of 8 Sep 2026. PubPeer could not be reached (403), so PubPeer threads were not checked.

Grades (grade the study, never the speaker): **A** meta-analyses / broad agreement across many studies; **B** controlled or field trials, or very large consistent cohorts; **C** smaller, narrower or mixed; **D** what experienced practitioners do ahead of research; **E** unproven.

## 4. PRACTICES THE EVIDENCE SUPPORTS — with a scheduling shape

### Numbers the app may quote verbatim (verified on a landing page or registry record this pass)
- Auto-enrolment: participation 37% → 86% (Madrian & Shea, secondary); UK 55% → 88% of eligible employees 2012→2021 (DWP official statistics).
- SMarT: 3.5% → 13.6% over 40 months, 78% joined, 80% stayed through four pay rises (Thaler & Benartzi 2004).
- Long-run shrinkage: +0.6% of income (auto-enrol), +0.3% (auto-escalate) (Choi et al. 2024).
- Denmark: $1 of subsidy → ≈1c new saving; 85% passive savers (Chetty et al. 2014).
- Commitment account: +82% balances at 12 months, take-up 28% (Ashraf et al. 2006, J-PAL page).
- Future-self photo: 1.5% → 1.7% one-off contributions, n≈50,000 (Robalino et al. 2023).
- Fresh start: +1.3–1.4 pp delayed take-up; +17–19 bp contribution rate over 8 months, N=6,082 (Beshears et al. 2021).
- Reminders in the US: +0.05 pp average, +0.13 pp best arm, n≈2m (Milkman et al. 2025).
- Progress monitoring: d = 0.40, 138 RCTs, N=19,951 (Harkin et al. 2016).
- Ego depletion: d = 0.04 (23 labs, N=2,141) and d = 0.06 (36 labs, N=3,531).
- Nudges: d = 0.45 uncorrected (Mertens) → no evidence after bias correction (Maier); nudge units +1.4 pp vs papers +8.7 pp (DellaVigna & Linos).

### Numbers NOT to quote until verified on the paper itself
- Karlan et al. 2016 "6% / 16%"; Kaiser et al. 2022 "0.2 SD / 0.1 SD"; per-study n for Bryan & Hershfield 2012, Soman & Cheema 2011 (146 is secondary), Gargano & Rossi 2024, Hershfield–Shu–Benartzi 2020; any figure from the DWP 2019 evaluation PDF; any "people who set deadlines perform better" claim (source retracted).
