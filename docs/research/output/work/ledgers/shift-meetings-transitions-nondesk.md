# Verified source ledger — Work round, slice 3
## Shift work as work · Meetings · Return-to-work and career transitions · Non-desk work

Compiled 2026-09-08 for the IntentNorth Work & Leadership round
(`docs/research/BRIEF-work.md`).

**Verification method.** Publisher landing pages return 403 to this session
almost universally, so every row below was checked at *record* level:

- **Crossref REST** (`https://api.crossref.org/works/<doi>`) for title,
  container title, year, volume/issue/page, author list, work type, and the
  `update-to` / `updated-by` relations that carry retractions, corrections
  and errata.
- **Europe PMC REST**
  (`https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=...&resultType=core`)
  for the PubMed record mirror — PMID, PMCID, publication-type tags and the
  structured abstract, which is where most of the numbers quoted below come
  from.
- **PMC / Europe PMC full text** where an open-access PMCID exists; noted
  per row when full text was read rather than the abstract alone.
- **Agency and registry pages** (IARC, Safe Work Australia, Fair Work
  Ombudsman, NHVR, Cochrane) read directly where they serve plain HTML.

**Status labels.** VERIFIED = every number in the "key result" column was
read on a record I opened, and the bibliographic details match Crossref.
PARTLY VERIFIED = the record and design are confirmed but at least one
number comes from the abstract only, or a number widely attributed to the
paper could not be confirmed at record level and is flagged inline.
UNVERIFIED = could not confirm at record level; flagged, and not usable to
set a grade.

**Grades apply to the study, never the speaker.** A = meta-analyses or
broad agreement across designs; B = controlled trials, or very large and
consistent cohorts; C = smaller, narrower or mixed; D = experienced
practitioner practice running ahead of the research; E = unproven.

### Not to be written

- **Anything that tells a night worker their cancer risk.** IARC classified
  the *hazard* and says in its own Q&A that this does not indicate the level
  of risk. A card that says "night work is a probable carcinogen" without
  the second half is both frightening and wrong.
- **Any personal-practice card framed as a fix for a bad roster.** Wong,
  Dawson, Pandalai & Sprajcer (2025) put everything an app can offer in the
  bottom two tiers of the hierarchy of controls. The coach should say that
  plainly and kindly, exactly as the brief asks it to for burnout.
- **A FIFO mental-health card.** Two Australian samples from the same period
  point in opposite directions (Gilbert 2023 vs Asare 2021). Route to
  support, never characterise how the person feels.
- **A shift-worker sleep card of any kind.** Already written and graded by
  the recovery round; duplicating it would be worse than leaving the gap.
