# Positioning review — why people cannot tell what we sell

> **Superseded in part, 2026-09-04.** The problem statement and the film script
> below were rewritten after Isaac's review: the four-apps opener is not the
> customer's problem, and counts of practices come out in favour of the
> evidence grading. `docs/PROBLEM_STATEMENT.md` is the reference for the

Written 2026-09-04 after user feedback that neither the site nor the film
explains what IntentNorth is or solves. Reviewed cold, as a visitor who has
never heard of it, against the built site at the current commit and the
two films (the coaching cut and the new "built with intent" cut, which is
mine and fails the same test).

## The verdict

The site and the films describe how the product works to people who
already know what it is. Nobody arriving cold is told, in plain words,
what it is, who it is for, what problem it removes, or what happens when
they install it. Everything on the page is true. Almost none of it is
understandable on first contact.

Three facts, measured from the build:

| | Desktop | Mobile |
|---|---|---|
| Page height | 16,682px, about 18 screens | 24,906px, about 30 screens |
| Words | 3,098 | 3,098 |
| Headlines | 27 | 27 |

The first viewport does not contain the word "app" or "iPhone". The
category label above the headline is "THE PERSONAL OPERATING SYSTEM",
which is a category no buyer has ever searched for.

## The five-second test, as a visitor sees it

**Headline:** "Seven coaches. One life, built with intent."
**Sub-line:** "Your results should change what happens next. Each coach
works from the same profile, adapts to the week you actually have, and
tells you why it changed."
**Button:** "Build my profile"
**Visual:** a mock card reading "Upper A — main work stays, accessories
rest today · READINESS against your own baseline · HRV and resting heart
rate below your own baseline, not a population band · Protein anchor
165–198g · kitchen closes 7:30pm · The slip window is after 10pm".

What a cold visitor can answer after five seconds:

- *What is it?* Unclear. "Coaches" reads as people. "Profile" reads as a
  form. Nothing says software, phone, or app.
- *What problem does it solve?* Not stated. "Your results should change
  what happens next" is a belief, not a problem.
- *Who is it for?* Not stated.
- *What happens if I press the button?* Unknown. It is not the app.
- *What does it cost?* Screen 17.

The hero visual makes it worse. It shows the densest, most expert lines the
app can produce (HRV baselines, protein anchors in grams, "Upper A", "slip
window") to someone who does not yet know it plans a week.

## Why the site fails

1. **It sells the engine, not the outcome.** Readiness, back-off, phased
   weeks, ladders, levels, learning loops, shared-profile plumbing. The
   website session's own motion brief diagnosed this ("we have been
   advertising the engine") and the fix still argues mechanisms.
2. **Every headline is an aphorism.** "Moving time is easy. Changing the
   program is valuable." "Answer once. Context compounds." "Credibility
   lives in the source — and the limitation." "Nothing would tell me what
   to change." These reward people who already understand and read as
   riddles to everyone else. Twenty-seven of them in a row is a manifesto,
   not a product page.
3. **The page is eighteen screens long.** A product page earns the second
   screen by making the first one clear. This one asks for eighteen before
   the price.
4. **The navigation is internal vocabulary.** Difference · Behaviour ·
   Pathways · Science. A visitor's questions are What is it · How does it
   work · What does it cost · Is it on my phone.
5. **The call to action asks for work before understanding.** "Build my
   profile" is a five-minute questionnaire on the website, not the app. A
   visitor who does not yet know what the product is will not start it.
6. **It speaks to three audiences at once.** The evidence scientist ("we
   publish the 104 we grade C or weaker"), the lifter ("Bench 4 × 6 at
   90kg"), and the parent ("Saturday 9:30 — the short loop, sized for the
   youngest"). Each paragraph switches. None of them is told they are the
   person this is for.
7. **Our words, not theirs.** Protocols, pathways, coaches, ladder, rungs,
   readiness, arbitration, operating profile, level card, the block. See
   the glossary below. A visitor uses none of these.
8. **The proof is buried.** The screenshots are on screen eleven. The
   one thing that would make it concrete, a real Today screen with a plain
   caption, is not in the first viewport; a stylised mock card is.

## Why the films fail, including mine

- **No problem, no category.** Both open on a slogan ("A coach asks before
  they prescribe"; "A week built with intent"). Neither says what the
  product is before showing it working.
- **Chapter titles are our positioning, not their language.** "Proven
  protocols, synthesised and rated" is what we say in the boardroom. A
  viewer hears "protocols" and thinks hospitals.
- **The screens are unreadable at speed.** The phone shows dense, real UI
  for 1.5 seconds per beat. Real is right; dense is not. The film should
  show one line per screen and let the rest blur.
- **No person, no outcome.** Nobody's Tuesday gets better on camera. The
  competitors we studied all show what life looks like after.
- **Too many ideas.** Seven coaches, 177 practices, grades, safety lines,
  sleep debt, arbitration, levels and ladders in 47 seconds. A cold viewer
  retains one.

## The glossary: what we say, what they hear, what to say instead

| We say | They hear | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| Personal operating system | Nothing, or a computer | An app that plans your whole week |
| Seven coaches | Seven people | Seven areas of life: training, food, sleep, habits, money, work, family |
| Protocols | Hospitals | Practices, or habits with the evidence behind them |
| Pathways | Hiking | Programs, one per area |
| Profile | A form | Tell it about your life once |
| Ladder, rungs, levels | Games | It gets harder as you progress |
| Readiness, HRV baseline | A wearable I do not own | A short night changes today's session |
| Arbitration, displaced, "the order you set" | Legal | It moves things and tells you why |
| Build my profile | Homework | Download on the App Store / See how it works |
| Phased weeks, block, deload | Gym jargon | A four-week training plan built from your own lifts |

## The message we should be sending, in order

1. **What it is.** IntentNorth is an iPhone app that plans your training,
   food, sleep, habits, money, work and family time into one week, and
   re-plans it when life happens.
2. **Who it is for.** People with a full life who are tired of apps that
   each know one part of it.
3. **The problem.** A gym app, a sleep app, a budget spreadsheet, a family
   calendar. None of them talk to each other, so the plan breaks the first
   bad week.
4. **What you get,** in three tangible things: a day that is already
   decided when you open it, with the reason for each item; programs for
   the seven areas that get harder as you progress; a library of 177
   practices with the evidence grade and a safety line, one tap to add.
5. **Why to trust it.** Nothing you enter leaves your phone. Support for
   your hardest moments is free forever. We publish the evidence grade,
   including the weak ones. Made in Brisbane.
6. **What it costs.** Free to start (the interview, your first insight,
   the day's shape). Plus is $14.99 a month, $89.99 a year or $249 once.
7. **What to do.** Download on the App Store. Until it is live: it is in
   App Store review, leave your email.

Everything else on the current page is either proof for one of these
seven or belongs on a second page called How it works.

## Rewrites

### Hero

**Option A (recommended).**
> **One app for the whole week.**
> Training, food, sleep, habits, money, work and family, planned into the
> hours you actually have and re-planned when life happens. For iPhone.
> [Download on the App Store] [See how it works]
> Free to start · Nothing leaves your phone · Made in Brisbane

**Option B.**
> **Stop running seven apps that don't talk to each other.**
> IntentNorth plans your training, food, sleep, habits, money, work and
> family into one week, and adjusts it when your week changes.

**Option C.**
> **Your week, planned. All of it.**
> One iPhone app for training, food, sleep, habits, money, work and family.
> It decides today for you and tells you why.

The visual beside it: the real Today screen, in a phone, with three items
visible and one caption: "Friday. Already decided. Every line says why."

### Navigation

How it works · The seven areas · The library · Pricing · Download.

### Screen two: how it works, in three steps

1. Tell it about your life once. Ten questions, two minutes.
2. It builds your week. Training, food, sleep, habits, money, work,
   family, placed into the days you have.
3. It adjusts and tells you why. A short night changes the session. A
   busy day moves the walk. Nothing is lost.

### The page, from eighteen screens to six

1. Hero: what it is, who for, download, one real screen.
2. How it works in three steps, with the three protocol frames or the
   Today screen.
3. The seven areas: seven tiles, one plain sentence each, one real screen
   each.
4. The library: grade, safety line, one tap. The honest grading is a
   trust line here, not a thesis.
5. Trust: privacy, free hardest-moment support, made in Brisbane, the
   founder in one paragraph.
6. Pricing, three questions people actually ask, download.

The evidence section, the behaviour model, the trajectory chart, the
category comparison and the shared-profile diagram move to How it works.

allowed to blur. The three positioning lines are still the spine (the
library, the living plan, the seven areas) but they are said in the
viewer's words and after the problem.

## How to check it before shipping

Show the first viewport to five people outside the project for five
seconds, then ask three questions: what is it, who is it for, what would
you do next. Pass is four of five saying "an app that plans my week" or
close. Run it on the current page first so the improvement is measured,
not assumed. Same test for the film with the first ten seconds.
