# The marketing engine

Decided 2026-09-04. The working surface is the **IntentNorth Outreach
Desk**, a shared page on a shared database:
https://claude.ai/code/artifact/0de392ba-ebd7-423e-a769-0c6346603200

It holds the pipeline (idea → researched → contacted → replied → meeting →
Foundation pilot → won / parked), a follow-up queue by date, the lead table
with a "verified" flag, an ideas board, the email drafts with merge fields,
and the playbook. Everyone who opens it sees the same rows.

## Positioning, in one breath

Australian-developed, Brisbane. One profile, seven coaches — training, food,
sleep, habits and urges, work, money, relationship and family — built into
the week a person actually has and rebuilt when it changes. Nothing entered
leaves the phone. Support for the hardest moments is free, permanently.

Numbers we may quote, all counted from the shipped code: 177 practices,
188 researchers and practitioners credited, 145 plain-language safety
lines, 16 behaviours, 7 spoken meditations, 5,376 distinct training
programmes. Never: outcomes we have not measured, testimonials that do not
exist, user numbers, "clinically proven".

## The Foundation offer

- **Foundation Workplaces** — the first 20 organisations. 90 days of Plus
  for up to 25 staff as App Store offer codes staff redeem themselves. No
  invoice, no procurement, no data back to the employer. In return: a
  30-minute conversation at day 30 and day 90, and permission to name
  them (logo optional).
- **Foundation Members** — clubs and networks. A code per member, 60 days,
  and a 20-minute talk at a session.
- Mechanics: App Store Connect → Subscriptions → Plus Yearly → Offer
  Codes → one-time-use batch per organisation (free, 3 months, new
  subscribers). Batches named for the organisation, so redemptions report
  by organisation with no personal data. Up to 1M redemptions per quarter,
  far beyond need.

## Segments and the first list

## The law, and the rules we set ourselves

- Spam Act 2003. A business address published for the role, and a message
  relevant to that role, is inferred consent. Personal addresses, scraped
  addresses and "no unsolicited email" pages are not. Every email
  identifies the sender (name, IntentNorth, Brisbane, ABN) and offers a way
  to say no that is honoured within five business days.
- Sent from the intentnorth.app address, one at a time or in small
  batches, personalised. No bought lists, no bulk cold-email tooling.
- Communities: give first. r/AusFinance and r/australia allow no
  promotion; answer questions with the method and never link. r/Brisbane
  and r/productivity allow one disclosed maker post with mod permission.
  Quora and Whirlpool: answer honestly, name three apps including ours,
  disclose. parkrun is not a channel.
- The website newsletter (Klaviyo, opt-in, unsubscribe in every email) is
  where consumer email lives. Nothing inside the app collects addresses.

## Cadence and measurement

Monday: ten leads verified and contacted. Wednesday: follow-ups due.
Friday: replies logged, pipeline reviewed, one idea promoted to "trying".
First follow-up at five business days, one more at twelve, then park.

Measured in the desk (sent, replies, meetings, pilots, won) and in App
Store Connect (redemptions per offer-code batch, conversions after the free
period). A reply rate under 5% after 40 sends means the draft, not the
list.
